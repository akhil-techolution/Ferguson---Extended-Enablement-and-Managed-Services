09 · Train-the-trainer

Building Ferguson’s own Gemini trainers

Train-the-trainer turns Ferguson employees into trainers for their own teams, so enablement keeps scaling inside Ferguson. It is part of Options 2 and 3.

  1. 1

    Techolution trains Ferguson trainers

    Selected Ferguson staff learn Gemini, the cohort workshop format and the How-To Agent, so they can teach it.

  2. 2

    Ferguson trainers enable their teams

    They run sessions and answer questions for their own teams and branches, alongside Techolution’s support.

  3. 3

    Enablement continues inside Ferguson

    Ferguson becomes more self-sufficient, relying less on outside support as new users join.
