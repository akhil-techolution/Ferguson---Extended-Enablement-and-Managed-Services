04 · Expansion plan

From request-based to proactive activation

Until now, users were activated when Ferguson identified and provisioned them. Going forward, Techolution takes the lead on readiness and activation for both tracks.

Until now

Inbound, request-based

Users were activated only after Ferguson identified them and set up their access, so the pace of the rollout depended on how quickly those requests came in.

→
Going forward

Outbound, proactive

Techolution identifies the right users, prepares their access and activates licenses once Ferguson approves. Each group then moves straight into structured training.
